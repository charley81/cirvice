# Cirvice

The shared language for the private neighborhood services marketplace.

## Language

**Current Resident**:
An individual aged 18 or older who uses a dwelling inside the defined neighborhood boundary as their usual home. The individual can be an owner, renter, or adult household member and does not need to be named on a deed or lease.
_Avoid_: Homeowner, property owner

**Email Verification**:
Confirmation that a person controls the email address for an Account. It does not prove legal identity or that the person is a Current Resident.
_Avoid_: Identity verification, resident verification

**Approved Member**:
A Current Resident who has completed Email Verification and has active permission to use the private marketplace.
_Avoid_: Verified resident, vetted member

**Facebook Launch Link**:
A temporary shared joining link distributed through the private neighborhood Facebook group during the initial launch. It supplies evidence of neighborhood eligibility without connecting a Facebook account to Cirvice.
_Avoid_: Facebook verification, Facebook sign-in

**Single-Use Invitation**:
A private joining link from an Approved Member to one Current Resident. Cirvice records the inviting member, but other members cannot see this relationship.
_Avoid_: Referral, endorsement

**Address Review**:
A membership path in which a person temporarily supplies their home address so Cirvice can confirm that the claimed home is inside the neighborhood boundary. Address Review does not prove legal identity or ownership of the home.
_Avoid_: Address verification, identity verification

**Annual Revalidation**:
The yearly confirmation by an Approved Member that they continue to be a Current Resident. It is a membership eligibility check, not a new identity check.
_Avoid_: Annual verification, identity recheck

**Former Resident**:
An individual who no longer uses a dwelling inside the neighborhood boundary as their usual home.
_Avoid_: Inactive resident

**Ended Membership**:
A non-disciplinary membership outcome for a person who is no longer a Current Resident. It removes private neighborhood access without marking the person as having violated a rule.
_Avoid_: Revoked Membership, banned member

**Membership Suspension**:
A temporary restriction that removes private marketplace access while eligibility is incomplete or a serious concern is reviewed. It is not a final decision that the member violated a rule.
_Avoid_: Ban, Membership Revocation

**Membership Revocation**:
A disciplinary decision that removes membership after Cirvice confirms serious or repeated rule violations. It is different from Ended Membership, which is a normal result of no longer being a Current Resident.
_Avoid_: Ended Membership, Membership Suspension

**Membership Appeal**:
A request for a second administrator decision after a membership restriction or rejection. It is a review of the original decision, not a new membership application.
_Avoid_: Complaint, new application

**Eligibility Attestation**:
A person's formal confirmation that they meet the membership rules and supplied accurate information. It is not proof of legal identity or exact age.
_Avoid_: Identity verification, background check

**Primary Administrator**:
The administrator with final authority over membership decisions and delegated administrator permissions.
_Avoid_: Owner, super admin

**Backup Administrator**:
An administrator delegated to process routine membership work and respond to urgent risks within the authority granted by the Primary Administrator.
_Avoid_: Primary Administrator, moderator

**Service Provider**:
An Approved Member who offers one or more services that they personally perform. A Service Provider represents only themselves, even if they own or work for a business.
_Avoid_: Business provider, vendor

**Provider Profile**:
The private member-facing profile that identifies one Service Provider and groups their published Service Listings. It is visible only while the Service Provider has at least one published Service Listing.
_Avoid_: Business profile, company profile, resident directory profile

**Service Listing**:
An offer from one Service Provider for a service, skill, or talent that they personally perform.
_Avoid_: Business listing, team listing, advertisement

**Listing Approval**:
An administrator decision that a version of a Service Listing appears to follow Cirvice's marketplace rules. It is not verification or endorsement of the Service Provider, service, credentials, insurance, safety, legality, or quality.
_Avoid_: Provider approval, verified listing, recommended service

**Service Category Policy**:
The counsel-reviewed classification that determines whether a type of service may be published in South Carolina and North Carolina and what conditions apply. A service not yet classified cannot be published.
_Avoid_: Ad hoc listing approval, broad lawful-services rule

**Allowed Service Category**:
A Service Category that may be published under Cirvice's ordinary listing rules without category-specific evidence.
_Avoid_: Approved provider, verified service

**Conditional Service Category**:
A Service Category that may be published only when its defined credential, insurance, disclosure, value-limit, or other requirements are satisfied.
_Avoid_: Allowed Service Category, recommended service

**Prohibited Service Category**:
A Service Category that Cirvice does not permit to be offered through the marketplace, even when a particular form of the service might otherwise be lawful.
_Avoid_: Unsupported category, pending category

**Provider-Supplied Credential Claim**:
Structured licence, certification, bond, or insurance information supplied by a Service Provider and explicitly not checked by Cirvice.
_Avoid_: Verified credential, approved credential, trusted-provider badge

**Service Publishing Restriction**:
A temporary disciplinary restriction that hides a member's Service Listings and prevents listing submissions without removing the member's other private marketplace access.
_Avoid_: Membership Suspension, Membership Revocation
